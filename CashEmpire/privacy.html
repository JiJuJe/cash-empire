PLAYER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Click The Cash uses when you play as a guest or sign in.

Playing and signing in

You can play without an account. A guest save may be stored in your browser using localStorage. If you choose to sign in, Click The Cash uses Google Sign In to authenticate your account. The game never receives or stores your Google password.

The backend uses a stable Google account identifier to recognize your account. You choose a public game username. You can sign out from the account menu.

Game and account data

Account data may include your chosen username, game progress, leaderboard progress, achievements, rebirths, premium entitlements, and account creation and last activity timestamps used by the backend.

The backend may process an IP address to limit abuse. Cloudflare also provides hosting, backend infrastructure, security, and database services. Google provides authentication.

Public leaderboard

Public leaderboard entries may show a player's username, rank, lifetime money, and rebirth count. The public leaderboard does not display Google email, real name, Google account ID, IP address, or payment details.

Cookies

Signing in may use secure session cookies required to keep you logged in. Guest play does not require an account session.

Payments

If premium payments are enabled later, card and payment method details are handled by the external payment provider. Click The Cash does not store card details directly. The game may store purchase status and premium entitlements needed to provide the purchased benefit.
